# Virtual assistant task brief templates (free, copy and adapt)

Eight plain-text templates for handing work to a virtual assistant: a general task brief, inbox rules, invoice entry, customer support replies, a weekly report, a social media brief, lead research and a week-one onboarding checklist. Copy a block, fill in the brackets and send it to your assistant. They work with any assistant, not only ours.

## 1. General task brief

```text
TASK: [one-line name]
GOAL: why this task matters and what it is for.
STEPS: 1) ... 2) ... 3) ... (how you do it today).
EXAMPLE: link or paste one finished version you are happy with.
TOOLS AND ACCESS: which apps, and which login or permission is needed.
RULES: tone, limits, words to avoid, who approves.
DEADLINE: date and time, and your time zone.
DONE LOOKS LIKE: what the finished work should include.
ASK ME WHEN: situations where you want a question, not a guess.
```

## 2. Inbox triage rules

```text
GOAL: keep the inbox to a short list that needs me.
ALWAYS FLAG TO ME: customers, money questions, legal, anything marked urgent, anyone on this VIP list: [names].
DRAFT FOR MY APPROVAL: replies to new enquiries, scheduling requests, supplier questions.
REPLY WITHOUT ASKING: receipts, "thanks" messages, meeting confirmations using the approved reply below.
ARCHIVE: newsletters, notifications, cold sales emails.
NEVER: send anything about money, contracts or complaints without my approval.
REPORT: a five-line summary at the end of each working day: handled, waiting for me, questions.
```

## 3. Invoice and bill entry SOP

```text
INBOX: all bills go to [email address]. One place only.
ENTER: supplier, invoice number, date, due date, amount, tax, category.
MATCH: check each bill against the order or contract. Flag any mismatch.
DUPLICATES: search supplier and invoice number before entering.
APPROVAL: bills over [amount] wait for my yes. Smaller bills are queued for payment.
FILE: attach the bill to the entry and name it [supplier-date-number].
WEEKLY NOTE: bills entered, bills waiting, bills with questions.
NEVER: press pay. I approve and release payments.
```

## 4. Customer support reply guide

```text
TONE: [friendly and short / formal / warm]. Three replies I am proud of: [paste].
NEVER SAY: [phrases].
TOP QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS: 1) Where is my order? 2) How do I change or cancel? 3) How do I contact you? [paste approved answers].
ESCALATE TO ME: refunds over [amount], legal threats, safety issues, public complaints.
RESPONSE TIME: first reply within [hours] on working days.
REVIEW: I read twenty replies each week and send notes.
```

## 5. Weekly report template

```text
WEEK OF: [date]
DONE: list of finished tasks, with hours used.
IN PROGRESS: what is under way and the expected finish.
BLOCKED: what is waiting for me, and what is needed.
QUESTIONS: anything unclear.
NEXT WEEK: the top three priorities.
HOURS: used this week, used this month, hours left.
```

## 6. Social media content brief

```text
PLATFORMS: [list]. POSTS PER WEEK: [number].
AUDIENCE: who we talk to and what they care about.
VOICE: three words, plus three posts we like.
TOPICS: [list of five themes] and topics to avoid.
FORMAT: caption length, hashtags, links, image rules.
APPROVAL: calendar sent by the [date] of each month. Nothing posts without my yes.
REPORT: monthly note on posts, replies and what worked.
```

## 7. Lead research brief

```text
IDEAL CUSTOMER IN ONE SENTENCE: [role] at [type of company] in [place].
MUST HAVE: [size, industry, signals].
EXCLUDE: [competitors, past customers, regions].
COLUMNS: company, website, contact name, role, why they fit, source, status.
FIRST BATCH: twenty leads for review before any outreach.
RULES: follow each platform's terms and local email law. No bulk blasts. Stop if someone says no.
```

## 8. Week-one onboarding checklist

```text
DAY 1: share one task, one example and the access needed, using a password manager and limited permissions.
DAY 2: review each result before it goes anywhere. Give notes in writing.
DAY 3: add a second small task.
DAY 4: switch to a sample check instead of reviewing everything.
DAY 5: ten-minute call or message: what went well, what was unclear, what is next.
END OF WEEK: update the written steps with every correction, then decide the hours for next month.
```
